========================================================
   HUONG DAN CAI DAT VA SU DUNG - THT DOCSTUDIO AI
========================================================

Cam on ban da tai THT DocStudio AI - phan mem AI sua loi
chinh ta, dich Han-Nom, hoan toan MIEN PHI.

--------------------------------------------------------
BUOC 1: CAI DAT PHAN MEM
--------------------------------------------------------
1. Bam dup chuot vao file "THT_DocStudio_AI_Setup.exe"
2. Neu Windows hien canh bao mau xanh "Windows protected
   your PC" -> bam "More info" -> bam "Run anyway"
   (day la canh bao binh thuong voi phan mem khong mua
   chung chi thuong mai, khong phai virus)
3. Lam theo man hinh cai dat: bam Next -> tick vao o
   "Tao icon ngoai Desktop" neu muon -> bam Install
4. Cai xong, mo phan mem tu icon vua tao tren Desktop
   (hoac tu Start Menu)

De GO CAI DAT sau nay: vao Windows Settings > Apps >
tim "THT DocStudio AI" > Uninstall. (Giong nhu go
CapCut, Photoshop binh thuong.)

--------------------------------------------------------
BUOC 2: LAY VA NHAP API KEY GEMINI (MIEN PHI)
--------------------------------------------------------
Phan mem can 1 "chia khoa" (API key) MIEN PHI cua rieng
BAN de goi AI Gemini cua Google. Moi nguoi dung 1 key
rieng, khong ai dung chung ai.

Cach lay key (khoang 2 phut):
1. Mo trinh duyet, vao dia chi:
   https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey
2. Dang nhap bang tai khoan Google (Gmail) cua ban
3. Bam nut "Create API key" (hoac "Tao API key")
4. Copy doan ma vua hien ra (dang: AIzaSy........)

Cach nhap key vao phan mem:
1. Mo THT DocStudio AI
2. Bam nut "Cai dat" (bieu tuong banh rang) o goc tren
3. O tab "Sua loi van ban", tim muc "Gemini"
4. Dan (Ctrl+V) key vua copy vao o "API key"
5. Bam "Kiem tra key" de chac chan key hoat dong (se
   bao "OK" neu dung)
6. Bam "Luu" o cuoi cua so

XONG! Tu do phan mem se tu dung key cua ban de sua loi
va dich Han-Nom.

--------------------------------------------------------
BUOC 3 (TUY CHON): CAI OLLAMA DE CHAY AI HOAN TOAN
                    MIEN PHI, KHONG GIOI HAN, OFFLINE
--------------------------------------------------------
Neu ban khong muon phu thuoc Google, hoac muon dung AI
khong gioi han so lan, co the cai them Ollama de chay AI
ngay tren may tinh (khong can mang, khong can key).

Yeu cau may tinh: nen co card do hoa (GPU) rieng, cang
nhieu VRAM cang tot (toi thieu 6GB de chay muot).

Cach cai:
1. Tai Ollama tai dia chi chinh thuc:
   https://ollama.com/download
2. Chon ban Windows, tai ve va cai dat nhu phan mem
   binh thuong (Next -> Next -> Finish)
3. Sau khi cai xong, mo PowerShell (bam nut Start, go
   "PowerShell", Enter), go lenh sau de tai 1 model AI
   ve may (khoang 4-5GB, can internet de tai 1 lan duy
   nhat, sau do dung offline hoan toan):

   ollama pull qwen2.5:7b

4. Cho tai xong, mo THT DocStudio AI > Cai dat > doi
   "Provider" thanh "ollama" > Luu

--------------------------------------------------------
BUOC 4 (NANG CAO, KHONG BAT BUOC): LLAMA.CPP
--------------------------------------------------------
Day la lua chon danh cho nguoi da quen thuoc voi lap
trinh/bien dich phan mem - KHONG can thiet cho da so
nguoi dung. Neu ban da tu build san mot server llama.cpp
rieng, chi can vao Cai dat > tab "Sua loi van ban" >
muc "llama.cpp" > nhap dia chi server cua ban (vi du:
http://localhost:8080).

Neu chua biet llama.cpp la gi, cu bo qua buoc nay - dung
Gemini (Buoc 2) hoac Ollama (Buoc 3) la du dung tot roi.

--------------------------------------------------------
CAU HOI THUONG GAP
--------------------------------------------------------
H: Phan mem bao "API key chua sang" / loi ket noi?
D: Kiem tra lai da dan dung key chua, key co con dau

   khong.

H: Dung Ollama co ton tien khong?
